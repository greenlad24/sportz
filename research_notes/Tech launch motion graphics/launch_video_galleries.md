# Galleries and Directories of Real Tech / SaaS Product Launch Videos

Method note: the research used web search (about 16 calls). Direct page fetches of flowjam.com and echai.ventures failed because those hosts are blocked in this sandbox's network allowlist. Facts below therefore come from search-result snippets, Product Hunt listings and third-party directory pages, not live visits. Collection sizes and prices are as stated by those sources. They should be treated as approximate and checked live before relying on them.

## Which dedicated launch-video galleries exist?

### Takeaway
There are about 8 dedicated, browsable launch-video libraries. The largest and most current are the eChai Video Library (around 1,740 startup videos, with Linear, Notion, Stripe, Vercel and Raycast entries from 2024–2026), Scrollsnacks (500+ launch videos split into tagged hook/problem/CTA sections, updated each weekday) and Flowjam's free Launch Videos library. Several smaller Product Hunt-era libraries (Startup Launch Videos 2022, Explainers 2018, Product Video Explorer 2023) look small or stale. Many of the libraries are run by video agencies as lead magnets.

### Cited Findings

**eChai Video Library — https://echai.ventures/videos (launch filter: https://echai.ventures/videos?category=launch)**
- A curated, founder-recommended collection of startup videos. You can browse by purpose (launch, explainer, founder story, pitch, ad, keynote, "AI-made"), industry and region, and videos are ranked by how many founders recommend them. The page reports about 1,740 videos. — [eChai search snippet](https://echai.ventures/videos?category=launch)
- Category pages exist for launch, explainer, keynote, pitch and ai_made. — [eChai explainer](https://echai.ventures/videos?category=explainer), [keynote](https://echai.ventures/videos?category=keynote), [ai_made](https://echai.ventures/videos?category=ai_made)
- It includes big-tech-SaaS films: "Introducing Linear Releases" (Linear, 2026) — [eChai](https://echai.ventures/videos/linear-introducing-linear-releases); Vercel Ship 2024 keynote — [eChai](https://echai.ventures/videos/vercel-vercel-ship-2024-keynote); Stripe Sessions 2024/2025 keynotes — [eChai](https://echai.ventures/videos/stripe-stripe-sessions-2025-product-keynote); Notion "Meet Notion Calendar", "Notion AI is here for everyone", Make with Notion 2025 keynote — [eChai](https://echai.ventures/videos/notion-make-with-notion-2025-product-launch-keynote); Raycast AI tutorial 2025 — [eChai](https://echai.ventures/videos/raycast-raycast-ai-tutorial-2025)
- It has many YC launch videos (Palmier YC S24, Opine YC W25, InsForge, Vibeflow, Artificial Societies). — [eChai Palmier](https://echai.ventures/videos/palmier-palmier-yc-s24-launch-video), [eChai Opine](https://echai.ventures/videos/opine-opine-yc-w25-launch-video)
- It also includes a "Clay beginner walkthrough 2026" entry, which shows 2026 content is being added. — [eChai](https://echai.ventures/videos/clay-clay-com-beginner-walkthrough-2026-step-by-step-guide)
- Free to browse (no paywall mentioned in snippets). Up to date (2026 entries).

**Scrollsnacks — https://producthunt.com/products/scrollsnacks**
- A searchable launch-video library that splits each video into timed sections tagged hook, problem, solution, feature, proof and CTA. The founder built it after repeatedly scrubbing through launch videos to check hook length and when the product first appears. — [Product Hunt](https://producthunt.com/products/scrollsnacks)
- Section tags: Hook, Problem, Solution, Feature, Benefit, Proof, Pricing, CTA, Outro. Each section describes the spoken lines, on-screen text and visual effects. A "Stitch" feature combines sections from different videos into a storyboard reel, and an MCP server lets AI assistants query the library. — [ProductCool](https://www.productcool.com/product/scrollsnacks)
- Over 500 launch videos and 4,000 tagged sections, "updated every weekday". — [HuntScreens](https://huntscreens.com/products/scrollsnacks)
- Recent launch (listed on Product Hunt as "launching today" in search). Pricing not found.

**Flowjam — "The Ultimate Library of Product Launch Videos" — https://www.flowjam.com/launch-videos**
- A curated collection of launch videos from top brands and startups, organized into software, app and hardware videos. — [Flowjam search snippet](https://www.flowjam.com/launch-videos)
- "Product Launch Videos 2.0" on Product Hunt: hand-picked SaaS launch videos, free with no email required. Maker: Adam Petty. — [Product Hunt](https://www.producthunt.com/p/flowjam-product-hunt-videos/product-launch-videos-2-0); [hunted.space](https://hunted.space/product/product-launch-videos-2-0); [UI/UX Showcase](https://uiuxshowcase.com/resources/flowjam-free-library-of-product-launch-videos/)
- Flowjam is a launch-video agency ("Loved by 30+ YC startups"), so the library also works as a lead magnet. — [Flowjam](https://www.flowjam.com/use-case/product-hunt)
- Flowjam's blog also publishes 2026 roundups: "15 Product Launch Video Examples That Actually Worked (2026)" and "30 Best Launch Video Examples (2026)". — [Flowjam blog](https://www.flowjam.com/blog/product-launch-video-examples-2026), [Flowjam blog](https://flowjam.com/blog/30-best-launch-video-examples-checklist)

**Product Video Examples — https://productvideoexamples.com**
- A hand-curated library of product videos tagged by length, type and industry; "100+ and growing" SaaS product videos. — [Product Hunt](https://www.producthunt.com/posts/product-video-examples); [BetaList](https://betalist.com/startups/product-video-examples)
- Pricing sources conflict. xpay.sh (May 2026) describes a free tier with 100+ videos plus a lifetime plan sold through sales contact. SpotSaaS and SaaSworthy list a one-time license with no free trial. No dollar price was found. — [xpay.sh](https://www.xpay.sh/saas-pricing/product-video-examples/); [SpotSaaS](https://www.spotsaas.com/product/product-video-examples/pricing); [SaaSworthy](https://www.saasworthy.com/product/product-video-examples/pricing)

**Startup Launch Videos (Product Hunt) — https://www.producthunt.com/products/startup-launch-videos**
- "A collection of the best videos used by startups to launch on Product Hunt". Launched 11 Sept 2022, about 130 followers. Appears tied to Flowjam. — [Product Hunt](https://www.producthunt.com/posts/startup-launch-videos); [Product Hunt alternatives](https://www.producthunt.com/products/startup-launch-videos/alternatives)

**Product Video Explorer (Gemoo) — https://www.producthunt.com/posts/product-video-explorer**
- 100+ hand-selected product videos from successful Product Hunt launches, plus customizable templates. Launched 11 Aug 2023, 149 followers. Maker: Morgan Kung (Gemoo). Includes templates, so mark it as part-template. — [Product Hunt](https://www.producthunt.com/products/product-video-explorer/makers)

**Explainers — https://www.producthunt.com/products/explainers**
- 130 startup videos in 7 categories (explainers, crowdfunding, commercials, educational, tutorials, customer/company testimonials). Launched 12 June 2018. Probably stale. — [Product Hunt](https://www.producthunt.com/products/explainers)

**LaunchTeaser (Peerlist) — https://peerlist.io/sheriff/project/launchteaser**
- A curated collection of product launch videos in categories such as Agtech, Devtools, Martech, AI, SaaS and Traveltech. Size and status unknown. — [Peerlist](https://peerlist.io/sheriff/project/launchteaser)

**Awesomic Launch Video Report — https://www.awesomic.com/launch-video-report (gated)**
- An analysis of 250 launch videos from Monday.com, Perplexity, Asana and top YC startups. It is a lead-gen form, and the report is delivered by email. Its headline stats look like loosely sourced marketing. — [Awesomic](https://www.awesomic.com/launch-video-report); [Awesomic](https://www.awesomic.com/free-launch-video-report)

**Agency blog "galleries" (roundups, not libraries)**
- Advids publishes many 30-example lists: SaaS product launch videos, launch teasers (updated June 2025), technology product launch videos, motion-graphics launch videos. — [Advids](https://advids.co/blog/saas-product-launch-videos), [Advids](https://advids.co/blog/30-top-motion-graphics-launch-video-examples-to-ignite-curiosity), [Advids](https://advids.co/blog?cat=saas&page=2)
- Vidico: a free swipe file of 100+ motion graphics videos, filterable by industry. — [Vidico](https://vidico.com/news/motion-graphics-examples/)
- Others: Moonb "12 product launch videos, sorted by format" — [Moonb](https://www.moonb.io/blog/product-launch-video); B2W "33 best animated product videos" — [B2W](https://www.b2w.tv/blog/best-animated-product-video-examples); VidPros "11 examples" — [VidPros](https://vidpros.com/product-launch-video/); Tella SaaS demo-video examples (demos, not launches) — [Tella](https://www.tella.com/examples/demo-video/saas); Speedrun (a16z) Substack "How to Make a Viral Launch Video" — [Substack](https://speedrun.substack.com/p/how-to-make-a-viral-launch-video?open=false)

### Inferences
- For reference on brands like Linear, Notion, Stripe, Vercel and Raycast, eChai looks like the most comprehensive and current free library. Scrollsnacks is the most structured one (section-level breakdowns, an MCP API).
- Most dedicated libraries are run by video agencies (Flowjam, Awesomic, Advids, Vidico). Their curation leans toward the SaaS/YC explainer-launch style rather than high-end brand films like Apple's.
- The older Product Hunt-era collections (Explainers 2018, Startup Launch Videos 2022, Product Video Explorer 2023) are probably not actively maintained.

### Gaps
- Could not load flowjam.com or echai.ventures directly (sandbox network block), so exact current counts and freshness are unverified beyond snippets.
- Scrollsnacks pricing and live URL (own domain) were not confirmed.
- LaunchTeaser size and liveness are unknown.

## Which UI/design inspiration libraries have a video/motion section focused on product launches?

### Takeaway
No major UI inspiration library (Mobbin, Godly, Land-book, Lapa Ninja) turned out to have a dedicated launch-video section in this research. The nearest matches are an AI-video vendor's showcase (Motion.so "Made with Motion"), a "video sections" category on maxibestof.one, and Dribbble tags. Treat those as adjacent, not core.

### Cited Findings
- Motion.so "Made with Motion": a gallery of launch films and product demos with their prompts and workflows, pitched for Product Hunt launch assets and SaaS demo inspiration. It is a tool vendor's showcase of videos made with its own product, so it is adjacent to templates/tools. — [Motion.so](https://motion.so/made-with-motion)
- maxibestof.one has a "video" sections category (landing-page sections that use video). — [maxibestof.one](https://maxibestof.one/sections/video)
- Mobbin is a mobile and web UI reference library (screens and flows, not launch films). — [design roundup](https://tech-blog-final-rho.vercel.app/articles/50-best-design-inspiration-sites-2025)
- Lapa Ninja catalogs landing pages by category, including "product". — [Lapa Ninja](https://lapa.ninja/category/product); Landing.love is a web design showcase. — [Landing.love](https://www.landing.love/about)
- Product Hunt lists a product called "Motion Library". Its exact content was not confirmed. — [Product Hunt](https://www.producthunt.com/products/motion-library)
- Toools.design has a motion-designers section covering brand animations, studio reels and title sequences (a resource directory). — [Toools.design](https://toools.design/for/motion-designers)

### Inferences
- Landing-page galleries mostly capture embedded hero videos incidentally. None was found to be organized around launch videos.

### Gaps
- Searches returned nothing on whether Godly, Land-book, SaaS Landing Page or Mobbin have a dedicated video or motion filter. A direct site check is needed.

### Takeaway
No dedicated GitHub "awesome-launch-videos" list or Are.na channel turned up. The few Notion/Gumroad swipe files found are about launch tweets or Notion's own videos, not launch-video galleries.

### Cited Findings

### Gaps
- Are.na was not reachable through search results, so channels may exist but are unverified. No specific X list was found.

## Which official company pages host their launch film archives?

### Takeaway
Apple keeps the most complete official archive (Apple Events, back to 2007). Stripe Sessions and Vercel Ship have official session or keynote pages. Most SaaS companies (Linear, Raycast, Figma, Notion, OpenAI) appear to host launch films mainly on their YouTube/X channels and changelogs, which this research could not verify through search.

### Cited Findings
- Apple Events (video) podcast/archive: replays of most Apple events since 2007, presented as "browse the archive of past events". — [Apple Podcasts](https://podcasts.apple.com/us/podcast/id275834665); [sir-apfelot.de](https://sir-apfelot.de/en/apple-events-podcasts-app-58951/)
- The Apple Archive, an unofficial Vimeo-hosted collection of Apple ads, WWDC sessions and internal videos. — [MacSurfer/9to5Mac](https://www.macsurfer.com/redir.php?u=1154864)
- Stripe Sessions 2026 session pages host keynote and session video, e.g. opening remarks and product keynote, and "Inside the AI economy" with Guillermo Rauch. — [Stripe](https://stripe.com/at/sessions/2026/opening-remarks-and-product), [Stripe](https://stripe.com/sessions/2026/inside-the-ai-economy)
- Vercel Ship: event pages for 2025 and for Ship 26 London/Berlin, with session pages such as the closing keynote. — [Vercel Ship 2025](https://vercel.com/ship/2025), [Vercel closing keynote](https://vercel.com/ship/session/closing-keynote), [Vercel Ship London](https://vercel.com/ship/london/register)
- Linear "Introducing Linear Releases" (2026) is indexed on eChai, a third-party library. — [eChai](https://echai.ventures/videos/linear-introducing-linear-releases)

### Inferences
- The official archives are event-centric (keynotes). Short launch films for individual features tend to live on company YouTube/X accounts and changelog pages (e.g. linear.app/changelog, raycast.com/changelog), which is why aggregators like eChai and Scrollsnacks are useful.

### Gaps
- Searches did not confirm official video archives for Linear, Raycast, Figma (Config), Notion, OpenAI, Arc/The Browser Company, so these remain unverified. Figma Config and OpenAI DevDay likely have event pages, but none was retrieved in this session.
