# Motion design platforms, communities and studios that showcase tech-company launch motion graphics

Research date: 2026-11-20. Method note: direct URL verification via curl was blocked by this session's network proxy (CONNECT 403 for essentially every host), and WebFetch could not resolve several hosts (e.g. studiohuncho.com, biddyco.com). URLs below are therefore either (a) URLs that appeared as live results in web searches run today, or (b) explicitly flagged as "unverified / constructed". Many client claims come from third-party "best agencies" listicles, which are flagged as lower reliability.

## Which platforms have specific tags/channels/collections for product launch or tech promo motion graphics?

### Takeaway
No major platform verifiably has an official "tech product launch" motion category; discovery happens through general motion categories (Behance Motion Graphics field, Dribbble tags/collections, Vimeo channels, Motionographer Quickies, Stash) combined with search terms like "product launch" or brand names. Dribbble is the one platform where a "product_launch" tag URL and user "Promo Video" collections were confirmed to exist.

### Cited Findings
- **Dribbble** — tag page `https://dribbble.com/tags/product_launch` and search page `https://dribbble.com/search/product-launch-video` both responded (HTTP 202, bot-challenge, i.e. pages exist) during testing in this session (no external citation; direct observation). User-curated collections exist, e.g. a "Promo Video" collection — [Dribbble collection 5342109 "Promo Video"](https://dribbble.com/benhaynes/collections/5342109-Promo-Video).
- **Behance** — has a general "Motion Graphics" creative field ("Featured In Motion Graphics" badges); no dedicated "product launch" tag was found. Recommended path: Behance search "product launch" filtered to the Motion Graphics field — [Behance profile example showing Motion Graphics field](https://www.behance.net/denisp10/info); [Fresno State Behance guide](https://careercenter.fresnostate.edu/resources/behance/). Studios post case studies there, e.g. [Ordinary Folk on Behance](https://www.behance.net/gallery/76929583/Ordinary-Folk). Constructed (unverified) browse URL: `https://www.behance.net/search/projects/product%20launch?field=motion%20graphics`.
- **Motionographer** — "Quickies" are short sidebar posts ("quick blasts of inspiration and news"), a large archive of brand spots, idents, reels; browse at [motionographer.com/quickie](https://motionographer.com/quickie). Posting criteria page: [Motionographer posting criteria](https://dev.motionographer.com/posting-criteria/amp/). Example tech/mobility quickie: [Line Mobility by Art&Graft](https://motionographer.com/quickie/line-mobility-artgraft/).
- **Stash Media (stashmedia.tv)** — covers studio work for tech brands, e.g. [Buck "The Gap" for Google Cloud](https://www.stashmedia.tv/buck-narrows-the-gap-google-cloud/), [The OpenAI Brand Refresh in Motion](https://stashmedia.tv/?p=109761), [Wix Motion Studio Launches Symphony](https://www.stashmedia.tv/?p=122261), [Art&Graft for Line Mobility](https://www.stashmedia.tv/?p=117580). Has per-studio tag pages, e.g. [stashmedia.tv/tag/manvsmachine](https://www.stashmedia.tv/tag/manvsmachine/page/12).
- **Vimeo** — studios host launch films there, e.g. [Ordinary Folk × Google Gemini app launch](https://vimeo.com/913062540), [Ordinary Folk Reel II](https://vimeo.com/803180408), [Techless launch video by Ordinary Folk](https://vimeo.com/390572049), [an "Anthropic" video on Vimeo](https://vimeo.com/1164510643). Staff Picks channel (`https://vimeo.com/channels/staffpicks`) is the standard curated channel, but no search result in this session confirmed a tech-launch-specific Vimeo channel.
- **Codrops** — runs a recurring "Motion Highlights" roundup and a motion-design tag: [tympanus.net/codrops/tag/motion-design](https://tympanus.net/codrops/tag/motion-design/).
- **It's Nice That** — covers studio launch/brand films (e.g. ManvsMachine Nike work and studio site updates) — [It's Nice That: ManvsMachine new site](https://www.itsnicethat.com/articles/manvsmachine-website-updates).

### Inferences
- The most efficient "collections" for tech launch films are studio-specific feeds (Stash tag pages, studio Vimeo accounts) rather than platform-wide tags.
- For Behance/Dribbble, brand-name searches (e.g. "Figma", "Gemini", "Stripe") inside the motion field likely surface more launch work than a "product launch" tag.

### Gaps
- Could not verify existence/contents of: Muzli, Collectif, The Brand Identity motion section, Awwwards video section, Are.na channels for launch videos, Pinterest boards, Motion Design School showcases, Vimeo motion graphics category page. Searches returned nothing specific and direct fetches were network-blocked.
- No confirmed official "product launch" tag on Behance.

## Which studios specialize in tech launch films and which famous launch videos did they make?

### Takeaway
The best-documented tech launch/brand film studios are Buck (Apple iMac Pro "Power to the Pro", Apple TV keynote opener, Google Cloud), ManvsMachine (Apple iMac Pro, Google I/O ARCore), Ordinary Folk (Google Gemini app launch), Oddfellows (Figma "This is Figma" anthem; Google, Replit, Discord), Giant Ant (long-running Slack campaign; Buck-owned since 2022), Animade (Google, Slack; acquired by Duolingo), Studio Dumbar/DEPT (OpenAI brand film), and basement.studio (Vercel Ship, Linear client). Credits for Anthropic launch films beyond Mother's "Keep Thinking" campaign could not be confirmed.

### Cited Findings
**Buck** (LA/NY/Amsterdam/Sydney) — buck.co
- Apple iMac Pro "Power to the Pro" art film; won a Clio and Gold/Silver Tellys — [Buck case study](https://buck.co/work/apple-power-to-the-pro).
- Apple TV keynote opening title sequence with Media Arts Lab — [yeahok.cool project page](https://www.yeahok.cool/project/openingfilm).
- Awards for Apple "Share Your Gifts" and "Wonderful Tools" — [Wikipedia: Buck](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buck_(design_company)).
- Google Cloud "The Gap" brand film, CD Gareth O'Brien — [Stash](https://www.stashmedia.tv/buck-narrows-the-gap-google-cloud/).
- Best browse URL: `https://buck.co/work` (constructed from case-study URL pattern).
- Claim that Buck did Anthropic's Claude Opus 4.6 launch appeared only in a search-engine summary; a dedicated follow-up search found no source — treat as UNVERIFIED.

**ManvsMachine** (London/LA; part of The Landor Group) — manvsmachine.co.uk
- One of six artists for Apple's iMac Pro film — [squarespace-hosted credit page](https://ranunculus-squid-g9ht.squarespace.com/work/specialists-nnsb7); studio profile — [Motion Array Studio Spotlight](https://motionarray.com/learn/motion-design/studio-spotlight-manvsmachine/).
- Google I/O 2019 ARCore developer-release work (secondhand freelancer credit) — [Contra: Peter Tarka](https://contra.com/petertarka).
- Primarily Nike (Air Max Day, Flyknit, Mercurial) — [It's Nice That](https://www.itsnicethat.com/articles/man-vs-machine-nike-flyknit-innovation-190416).

**Ordinary Folk** (high-end motion studio)

- Techless launch video — [Vimeo](https://vimeo.com/390572049); reel — [Vimeo](https://vimeo.com/803180408); studio podcast — [School of Motion](https://schoolofmotion.com/blog/ordinary-folk-podcast).

Also Dropbox, Headspace — [biddyco](https://www.biddyco.com/blog-posts/best-motion-agencies). (All third-party listicles.)

Founding year conflicts across sources (2007/2009/2010).

- Art&Graft — Line Mobility launch — [Motionographer](https://motionographer.com/quickie/line-mobility-artgraft/).

### Inferences
- Apple's iMac Pro film is a useful "collection" itself: it commissioned multiple studios (Buck, ManvsMachine and others), so each studio's case page links to the same launch.
- AI-lab launch work (OpenAI, Anthropic) increasingly goes to branding agencies (Studio Dumbar/DEPT, Mother) rather than pure motion studios, and much is made in-house (OpenAI hires its own motion designers — [job listing](https://jobs.therundown.ai/jobs/195170318-motion-designer)).

### Gaps
- Unverified/not found: Jamm, Gretel (search found only GRETEL Studio Karlsruhe and an unrelated JAM Studio Vietnam — likely not the intended studios; the NY studio "Gretel" was not confirmed), Gigantic Studio, Hobbes, Moth Studio, Breakpoint. No credits found linking them to tech launches.
- Who produced Linear, Notion, Stripe, Figma (Config keynote films) and OpenAI product launch videos remains largely unconfirmed; many are in-house.
- Rive "Made with Rive" not researched (code/tool-adjacent, out of scope per assignment).

## Which newsletters/blogs regularly feature tech launch motion graphics?

### Takeaway
Regular motion-focused editorial sources are Motionographer (incl. Quickies/weekly), Stash Media, School of Motion's Motion Mondays (100k+ subscribers), Codrops Motion Highlights, Val Head's UI Animation Newsletter, and It's Nice That; none is tech-launch-specific.

### Cited Findings
- Motion Mondays (School of Motion) — weekly, 100,000+ subscribers, tutorials/jobs/studio news — [schoolofmotion.com/newsletter](https://schoolofmotion.com/newsletter).
- UI Animation Newsletter by Val Head; Motionographer Weekly — [Speckyboy](https://speckyboy.com/best-newsletters-videographers/); [Envato](https://elements.envato.com/learn/video-newsletters).
- Codrops Motion Highlights — [Codrops](https://tympanus.net/codrops/tag/motion-design/).
- School of Motion "Great Sites for Motion Design Inspiration" list — [School of Motion](https://schoolofmotion.com/blog/great-sites-for-motion-design-inspiration-in-2021).
- Stash Media and It's Nice That regularly cover studio tech/brand films (see links above).

### Inferences
- Stash's brand/studio tagging makes it the best editorial archive for finding tech-brand motion work by studio.

### Gaps
- Muzli, Collectif, The Brand Identity, Creative Bloq, Reddit r/MotionDesign and X/Twitter threads could not be verified in this session.
